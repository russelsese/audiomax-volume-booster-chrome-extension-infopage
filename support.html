Support

If you need help with AudioMax, please contact the developer using the details below. I aim to respond within 48 hours.

For faster support, it helps to include:

  • Your Chrome version (found at chrome://settings/help)
  • The URL of the website where the issue occurred
  • A description of what you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Whether the issue occurs on all sites or just one

If you encountered a bug or want to leave feedback, you can also leave a review on the Chrome Web Store — I read every one.
